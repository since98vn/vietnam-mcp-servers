# Example questions for an AI assistant

Connect the servers first (see the README). Then ask in plain words.

## Address (2025 reform)

- "My customer list still says 'Phường Phú Cường, TP Thủ Dầu Một, Bình Dương'. What is the address now?"
- "Rewrite this delivery address to the new units: 123 Le Loi, Phuong Ben Thanh, Quan 1, TP HCM."
- "Which old provinces were merged into today's Tỉnh Tuyên Quang?"
- "List the communes of Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh after the reform."

## Payroll and tax

- "Gross salary 30,000,000 VND in Region I with one dependant: what is the net pay in November 2026?"
- "I want my employee to take home 25 million VND net. What gross should the contract say?"
- "What does a 40 million VND salary cost the company, with every contribution?"
- "What is the regional minimum wage in Region II from January 2027?"
- "Write 26,592,500 dong in words in Vietnamese for an invoice."

## Zalo business messaging

- "What do 10,000 ZNS order updates with a website button cost, by phone and by UID?"
- "We need the OA API with 8 staff accounts. Which Zalo OA plan is the smallest that fits?"
- "Can we send a Tag 3 promotion at 21:30 to 15,000 users today?"
- "What does Zalo error -133 mean?"
